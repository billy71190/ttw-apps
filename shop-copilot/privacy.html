Shop Copilot

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026. Take The Wheel LLC ("we", "us"). Contact: billy.redman@icloud.com

Shop Copilot helps you track receipts, send invoices, and plan for quarterly estimated taxes. It's built so your business data stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Shop Copilot has no account and no sign-up. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. We don't receive your receipts, invoices, clients, income, or tax numbers.

What stays on your iPhone

Receipts and their photos, invoices, clients, business profile and logo, tax payments, and settings are stored in the app's private storage, in a folder excluded from iCloud and device backups. Deleting the app deletes this data. Settings → Delete all data erases it immediately.

  • Receipt scanning uses Apple's on-device text recognition (Vision). Photos aren't uploaded.
  • Voice invoices use Apple's speech recognition. When your iPhone supports it, recognition runs on the device. Otherwise Apple may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy. Shop Copilot never stores or sends the audio.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.
  • App lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode through iOS. We never see biometric data.

When data leaves your iPhone (only when you choose)

  • Emailing or sharing an invoice, export, or backup. The PDF, CSV, or zip goes wherever you send it with Apple Mail or the share sheet.
  • Subscriptions (if offered). Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Children

Shop Copilot is for business use and isn't directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can export a backup or delete all data at any time in Settings.

Tax disclaimer

Tax figures in Shop Copilot are estimates only and not tax advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date.
