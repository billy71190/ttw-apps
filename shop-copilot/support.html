Help & Support

Getting started

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. Everything is stored on your iPhone.
Where is my data stored?
In the app's private storage on your iPhone, in a folder excluded from iCloud and device backups. Use Settings → Export backup to keep a copy somewhere safe.

Receipts

How does receipt scanning work?
Tap Snap or Photo. Shop Copilot reads the merchant, date, total, and tax on your phone with Apple's text recognition and suggests a Schedule C category. Check the fields, then tap Save.
How do I give receipts to my tax preparer?
Receipts → Export, pick a date range, then choose CSV (spreadsheet) or PDF (with photos) and share it.

Invoices

How do I make an invoice by voice?
Invoices → Say it, tap the mic and say something like “invoice John Smith 3 hours lawn care at 50 dollars due Friday”. Check what was filled in, then save.
How do clients get the invoice?
Open the invoice and tap Email invoice (Apple Mail with the PDF attached) or Share PDF. Mark it paid when the money arrives.

Quarterly taxes

Is the tax number exact?
No. It is an estimate only and not tax advice. Check with a tax professional or IRS Form 1040-ES.
Can Shop Copilot remind me before tax due dates?
Yes. Turn on Reminders in Settings. iOS asks for notification permission only then.

Backups and your data

How do I move to a new iPhone?
Settings → Export backup (.zip), save it to Files, then on the new iPhone use Restore from backup.
How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also deletes its data.
