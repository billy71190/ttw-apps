Getting started

What is VetClaim Buddy?

An organizing tool for your VA disability claim. It helps you see how well each part of a claim is documented, keep a symptom log, store evidence, track deadlines, write your personal statement, and put it all in one PDF. You stay in charge of your claim.

How do I start?

Open the Check tab and tap Add a condition. Type a condition you already plan to claim, answer the four questions about your evidence, and tap See evidence strength. Then use the other tabs (Symptoms, Evidence, Dates, Statement) to build your record.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no server account. Everything is stored on this iPhone.

The free Claim Strength Check

What does the Strength Check look at?

Four standard elements of service connection. For each one you say whether your evidence is documented, partial, or missing:

  • Diagnosis: a current diagnosis of the condition.
  • In-service event: an event, injury, illness, or exposure during service.
  • Medical link (nexus): a medical opinion connecting the condition to service.
  • Continuous treatment: ongoing symptoms or treatment over time.

The check is free and always will be.

What do Strong, Moderate, and Needs evidence mean?
  • Strong: diagnosis, in-service event, and medical link are all documented, and there is at least some record of ongoing symptoms or treatment.
  • Moderate: none of the first three elements is missing, but at least one is only partial (or ongoing treatment has no record yet).
  • Needs evidence: the diagnosis, in-service event, or medical link has no evidence yet.

These describe your paperwork only, not how VA will decide.

How do I read the “what's missing” list?

On your results, every element that is not fully documented is listed, weakest first, with examples of evidence that typically helps for that element. Use it as a checklist of records to look for, then save what you find in the Evidence tab.

Does it predict my rating or my chances?

No. It is educational only. There are no percentages, no odds, and no rating predictions. Only VA decides claims.

Body-system groups and bilateral conditions

Why are my conditions grouped?

The app sorts the conditions you entered into body-system groups that follow VA's Schedule for Rating Disabilities (38 CFR Part 4). It suggests a group from the name you typed, using a word list on your phone. You can pick a different group anytime with Edit on the condition. The groups are:

  • Musculoskeletal: upper extremities (38 CFR 4.71a, 4.73)
  • Musculoskeletal: lower extremities (38 CFR 4.71a, 4.73)
  • Spine and back (including neck) (38 CFR 4.71a (The Spine))
  • Mental health (38 CFR 4.130)
  • Ear and hearing (38 CFR 4.85-4.87)
  • Eye and vision (38 CFR 4.75-4.79)
  • Respiratory (including sleep apnea) (38 CFR 4.96-4.97)
  • Cardiovascular (38 CFR 4.100-4.104)
  • Digestive (38 CFR 4.110-4.114)
  • Genitourinary (38 CFR 4.115-4.115b)
  • Gynecological and breast (38 CFR 4.116)
  • Hemic and lymphatic (38 CFR 4.117)
  • Skin (38 CFR 4.118)
  • Endocrine (38 CFR 4.119)
  • Neurological (including migraines, TBI, and nerves) (38 CFR 4.120-4.124a)
  • Dental and oral (38 CFR 4.150)
  • Infectious diseases and immune disorders (38 CFR 4.88a-4.89)
  • Other / not sure

Grouping only organizes your own entries. The app never suggests conditions to add or predicts ratings.

How does the app know left or right?

If the name says left, right, L, R, or both (for example “left knee”), the app sets the side for you. You can change it with Edit.

What does the Bilateral badge mean?

The badge appears when you have entries for both the left and right arm, or both the left and right leg. When service-connected disabilities affect both arms or both legs (for example, both knees, or a left knee and a right ankle), VA applies a small extra amount called the bilateral factor before it combines ratings (38 CFR 4.26). VA applies this itself. This app does not calculate ratings or the bilateral factor.

A “Both sides” badge means you have entries for both sides of a paired organ. You have entries for both sides of a paired organ (such as eyes, ears, or kidneys). VA evaluates paired organs under its own rules, separate from the bilateral factor for arms and legs. This app does not calculate ratings.

The tools

Symptom log

In Symptoms, tap Log today to record the date, condition, severity, how often, flare-ups, and how it affected work and daily life. A chart and 30-day summary show the pattern over time.

Evidence locker

In Evidence, add records, photos, documents, provider opinions, and buddy statements. Tag each item to one or more conditions, add the date and source, and attach files or photos. Files are stored in the app's private storage.

Buddy statement guide

In Evidence, the buddy statement section has a plain guide you can send to someone who knows you, plus a tracker for who you asked and whether their signed statement arrived. When it arrives, add it as evidence so it is in your packet.

Intent-to-file countdown and reminders

In Dates, enter the date VA received your intent to file. The app counts down one year from that date and, if reminders are on, notifies you 90, 30, 7, and 1 day before. You can also add your own reminders. Always confirm exact dates with VA or your accredited representative.

Appointments

In Dates, add exams, doctor visits, or VSO meetings. If you choose, the app reminds you the day before and 2 hours before. Reminders are local notifications on this phone only.

Personal statement workspace

In Statement, pick a condition and answer guided prompts (when it started, what happened in service, symptoms now, effect on work and daily life, treatment). The app builds a draft in your own words that you can edit and share.

PDF packet

From the Settings screen, open PDF packet to create one organized PDF with your key dates, Strength Check breakdowns, statements, symptom log, evidence index with photos, and buddy statement tracking. It is made on your phone and only leaves when you share it. It is not a VA form.

Backup and restore

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings > Export backup (.zip). The zip has all your data and files. It contains health information, so save it somewhere private.

How do I restore a backup?

Go to Settings > Restore from backup and choose a VetClaim Buddy .zip. Restoring replaces everything currently on this phone, and the app asks you to confirm first.

Why not iCloud?

To keep your health information private, the app keeps its data out of iCloud and device backups. That means your own .zip export is the way to move to a new phone.

Privacy and where your data lives

Where is my data stored?

Only on this iPhone, in the app's private storage, in a folder excluded from iCloud and device backups. There is no account, no server, no ads, and no analytics.

Does anything leave my phone?

Only when you share a PDF, statement, or backup yourself. Nothing else leaves your phone.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings > Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Face ID and app lock

How do I lock the app?

Turn on Lock with Face ID or passcode in Settings (or during setup). The app then asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode each time you open it or come back to it, and hides its screen in the app switcher.

It says app lock is unavailable.

App lock needs a device passcode. Set one in iPhone Settings, then turn on the lock in VetClaim Buddy.

Subscriptions and Restore Purchases

Is the app free?

The Claim Strength Check is always free. Paid tools may not be available yet; right now every feature is free to use.

What would a subscription include?

If a subscription is offered, VetClaim Buddy Pro covers the symptom log, evidence locker, deadlines and reminders, personal statement workspace, and the PDF packet. It would be bought through the App Store with a flat monthly or yearly price.

Does the price depend on my claim?

No. The price is flat for everyone and is never tied to filing, outcome, award, or back pay, and never charged per claim.

How do I restore purchases or cancel?

When subscriptions are on, use Settings > Restore purchases on a new phone, and Manage subscription to cancel through your Apple account.

What the app does not do

What VetClaim Buddy does not do
  • It does not file, prepare, or submit claims or VA forms.
  • It is not a VSO, attorney, or claims agent.
  • It gives no legal or medical advice.
  • It never asks for your VA.gov, ID.me, or Login.gov sign-in.
  • It does not suggest conditions to claim or predict ratings.

For free help with your claim, find a VA-accredited Veterans Service Organization at va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation, or visit VA.gov.
