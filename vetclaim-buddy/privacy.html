VetClaim Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Publisher: Take The Wheel LLC. Contact: billy.redman@icloud.com.

VetClaim Buddy is an organizing tool for veterans preparing their own VA disability evidence. We are not VA-accredited and do not prepare, file, or represent your claim. We give no legal or medical advice. VetClaim Buddy is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

The short version

  • No account and no servers. You do not sign up, and we do not run a server that receives your data.
  • Your data stays on your iPhone. Conditions, Strength Check answers, symptom log, evidence photos and files, buddy statement tracking, deadlines, and statements are stored only in the app's private storage, in a folder excluded from iCloud and from iPhone/iTunes backups. Nothing is synced to iCloud.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sales. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We never ask for your VA.gov, ID.me, or Login.gov sign-in.

Information you enter

Everything you type or attach (conditions you choose to enter, answers, symptom entries, photos, documents, dates, statements, your name for the PDF) is health and personal information. It is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.

When data leaves your phone

Data leaves your device only when you choose to:

1. Share or export a PDF packet, a statement, the buddy statement guide, or a backup file through the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination (for example Files, Mail, or AirDrop). Backups contain health information; store them somewhere private.

Purchases

If subscriptions are offered, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on your device. Your price never depends on your claim's outcome or award.

Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications: reminders you set are scheduled locally on your iPhone.
  • Camera and Photos: used only when you choose to add a photo; selected images are copied into the app's private storage.
  • Face ID / passcode: optional app lock. Face ID is handled by iOS; we never receive biometric data.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Delete all data in the app, or delete the app. Because data is not backed up to iCloud or device backups, deleting the app permanently removes it unless you exported a backup yourself.

Children

VetClaim Buddy is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.

Contact

Take The Wheel LLC, billy.redman@icloud.com. Support requests are usually answered within an hour.
