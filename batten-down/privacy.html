Batten Down

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Last updated: November 21, 2026.

App: Batten Down for iPhone, published by Take The Wheel LLC.

Summary

Batten Down keeps your information on your device. We do not run servers for this app, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Information you enter

Your preparedness checklists, supply list, home and business inventory, photos, evacuation plan, contacts, check-in statuses, damage and claim log, and expenses are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. Photos are saved inside the app's own storage, not in your Photos library and not in iCloud Drive. Batten Down does not upload this information anywhere.

Your device may include the app's data in your own encrypted iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Apple controls those backups under its own privacy policy.

Location

If you tap "Use my current location", Batten Down asks for permission to use your location while the app is open. Location is used only to look up weather alerts. The app rounds your coordinates and sends them to the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) to get alerts for your area, and stores only that rounded point on your device. Batten Down does not track your location in the background.

If you include your location in an "I'm safe" text, it is added to a text message that you review and send yourself from Messages.

ZIP code and county lookups

If you look up alerts by ZIP code, the ZIP code is sent to zippopotam.us to find its approximate coordinates, which are then sent to the National Weather Service. If you choose a county, the county code is sent to the National Weather Service.

Contacts

If you tap "Add from Contacts" when adding a family contact or an adjuster, iOS shows its own contact picker. Batten Down does not ask for access to your contacts and cannot see your address book. Only the one person you pick (name, phone numbers and email) is passed to the app to fill in the form, and you can edit it before saving. It is saved only in the app's private storage on your iPhone, the same as a contact you type in, and is never uploaded.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access is used only when you add a photo to an inventory item, damage entry or receipt. Selected photos are copied into the app's private storage.

Sharing

PDFs, backups and texts leave your device only when you choose to share or send them using the iOS share sheet or Messages. Where they go after that is up to you and the app you share them with.

Third parties

Batten Down contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. The only network services it contacts are the National Weather Service (alerts) and zippopotam.us (ZIP lookups). Links to county, state, FEMA and Red Cross websites open in Safari and are governed by those sites' policies.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data erases everything the app has stored on your device. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Children

Batten Down is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Not an emergency service

Batten Down is not an official emergency service. Alerts may be delayed or unavailable. Always follow instructions from local officials. In an emergency, call 911.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy in the app and on our website. Questions: Take The Wheel LLC, billy.redman@icloud.com.
