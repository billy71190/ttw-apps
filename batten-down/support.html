Help & Support

What does Batten Down do?

Batten Down helps families and small businesses get ready for a hurricane and recover after one: prep checklists and a supply tracker, a home and business inventory with photos, an evacuation plan, family “I’m safe” check-in texts, a damage and claim log with expenses, and PDF reports you can send to your insurer.

Where are weather alerts from?

Alerts come from the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). You can look them up by your current location, a ZIP code or a county. Alerts can be delayed or unavailable. Batten Down is not an official emergency service. Always follow local officials, and in an emergency call 911.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter stays in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. There is no account and no Batten Down server. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open Settings and choose Export backup (.zip) under Your data. It includes your data and photos and opens in the share sheet so you can save it to Files or send it to yourself. On the new phone, install Batten Down and choose Restore from backup in Settings. Restoring replaces everything on that phone.

How do I send my inventory or claim log to my insurer?

Open Reports and make a Storm Packet, Inventory Report or Damage & Claim Log PDF. It opens in the share sheet so you can email, print or save it.

How do I delete my data?

Settings > Delete all data erases everything the app has stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app also removes its data.
