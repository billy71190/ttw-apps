Cliffmarch Support

Cliffmarch is a game by Take The Wheel LLC.

How to play

Tap March and choose a ridge on the Skyroad. The host runs on its own. Swipe left or right to steer. Walk through one of the two rune gates to grow or shrink the host. Pick up a Storm-Spire to fire ahead. Clash with the Emberpack (one for one). At the end of the road, fight the ridge’s warden. Amber earned from a taken ridge can be spent in The Forge on in-game upgrades. There are no in-app purchases.

The game is offline

Cliffmarch does not need a network connection, an account, or Game Center. Progress is saved on your device.

Lost progress

Progress lives only on that device. Deleting the app, or using the Reset progress control in Settings, clears it. There is no cloud backup.
