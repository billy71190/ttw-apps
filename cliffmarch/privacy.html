Cliffmarch Privacy Policy

Take The Wheel LLC · Effective date: 18 November 2026

Cliffmarch is a game that runs on your device. It does not collect, share, or sell personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Cliffmarch has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, and no network calls in normal play. The game is designed to work fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your progress, Amber total, Forge upgrades, and sound settings are saved locally on your device (in the app’s own storage). This data does not leave the device. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

Because we do not collect personal data, Cliffmarch does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update the date above and the copy in this page.
